# Completion day: the account and asset transfer checklist for buying a digital business

*By Joash Boyton, CEO of [Acquiry](https://www.acquiry.com/), Melbourne*

In a digital business, much of what the buyer pays for lives in online accounts. If those accounts do not move cleanly at completion, the buyer can own the business on paper while the seller still controls the domain, the code or the payments. A written transfer checklist, agreed before signing and worked through on the day, prevents most of these problems.

## Before completion

- **Build the inventory.** List every account the business uses, who owns it, the login email, the recovery email and phone, and whether two-factor authentication is tied to a personal device.
- **Mark the transfer route for each one.** Some accounts can change owner, some need a new account and a data migration, and some need the platform's or counterparty's consent.
- **Agree it in the documents.** Attach the inventory to the sale agreement and make delivery of each item a completion obligation.

## Domains and DNS

- Unlock each domain and obtain the transfer authorisation code, or push the domain to the buyer's registrar account.
- Record all DNS records before the move, including email authentication records, so nothing breaks during the transfer.
- Check renewal dates and any domains held privately by the seller that point to the business.

## Code, hosting and data

- Transfer repositories, including history and access tokens, and rotate every secret after completion.
- Move hosting, content delivery and database accounts, or migrate them, with a tested backup taken before the switch.
- Confirm where customer data sits and that its transfer is permitted under the business's privacy policy and the law that applies.

## Revenue and payments

- Payment processors, marketplaces and app stores often do not allow an account to be sold. Plan new accounts and approvals early.
- Agree how receipts that arrive after completion for sales made before it will be split.
- Update payout bank details only after the new owner has full control of the account.

## Marketing and audience

- Transfer email platforms and lists where consent allows, social profiles, advertising accounts and analytics properties.
- Keep historical analytics where possible. Buyers need the history to manage the business.

## After completion

- Change every password, revoke the seller's access and review user lists on every platform.
- Keep a short handover log of open issues and agree a period in which the seller will answer questions.

Earnings and price should be settled before any of this, for example with the free [SDE and EBITDA calculator](https://www.acquiry.com/tools/sde-ebitda-calculator/) and [digital business valuation multiples](https://www.acquiry.com/data/multiples/).
